Membership FAQ

Do I have to buy wine every month?

No. Your included bottles arrive each month. Anything extra is optional, at member prices.

Which wines get the member price?

All of them, including new releases. Members also get first access to small lots like the 20/20 Red Blend.

Can I cancel?

Yes, anytime.

Where do you ship?

To 37 states and Washington, D.C., free on orders over $50. An adult 21 or older must sign. Pickup at the vineyard is always free.

Can I give a membership as a gift?

Yes. Choose a level and a number of months, and we'll include a welcome card.
