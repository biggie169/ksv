Our heritage

Pennsylvania provenance, European heritage

Dr. Solomon and Wendy Luo came to Schuylkill County in 1983 with a vision for bringing the great European winemaking tradition to the county's rolling hills.

Raising their family over four decades in the region and growing roots in the community has symbolized the development of King Solomon's Vineyard into a sustainable winemaking operation serving the Schuylkill Valley.

Today we grow Cabernet Franc, Merlot and Chardonnay in the terroir of our own hillside, and make every wine by Old World methods. Every bottle traces its provenance to a single Pennsylvania vineyard.

Deep roots in Pennsylvania

From Orwigsburg to the vineyard

Dr. Solomon and Wendy Luo moved to Schuylkill County in 1983 with their three young children, Betsy, Caesar and Stella. They built a premier ophthalmology practice and raised their family in Orwigsburg.

A shared love of wine led Dr. Luo to plant vinifera, the classic European grapes few growers attempt in a climate this cool. King Solomon's Vineyard was founded in 2009.

Today we grow Cabernet Franc, Pinot Noir, Merlot, Shiraz, Chardonnay, Riesling and Pinot Grigio, among others. Every vine is tended by hand, and every wine is made and bottled on the estate.
