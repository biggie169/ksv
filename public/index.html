Our wine

Grown the old way, on our own land

Dr. Luo planted vinifera, the classic grapes of Bordeaux, Burgundy and the Rhine, in a climate where most growers would not try them. Growing them well here takes work that can't be rushed.

Estate grown
Every wine comes from our own vineyard, planted in 2010 on the hills above Pottsville.
Tended by hand
The vines are pruned, thinned and picked by hand, row by row, so only sound fruit reaches the winery.
Made on site
Fermented, aged and bottled at the vineyard in small lots. Nothing is sent out to be made elsewhere.

Questions

How membership works

Do I have to buy wine every month?

No. Your included bottles come every month, shipped or ready for pickup at the vineyard. Anything more is up to you, at member prices.

Which wines get the member price?

Every wine we sell, including new releases. Members also get first access to small lots such as the 20/20 Red Blend before they're offered to the public.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any month, from your account or by emailing us.

Where do you ship?

Shipping is free on orders over $50. We ship to 37 states and Washington, D.C., and an adult 21 or older must sign for delivery. You can also pick up at the vineyard in Pottsville.

Can I give a membership as a gift?

Yes. Choose a level and a number of months, and we'll send a welcome card with the first shipment.

What about the tasting room?

It's being finished now. Members get the first invitations, and Cellar and King's Table members get complimentary tastings for two.

Our story · Est. 2009

Over a decade in the making

A family that came to Schuylkill County in 1983, and a hunch that its hills could grow serious wine grapes.

Deep roots in Pennsylvania

From Orwigsburg to the vineyard

Dr. Solomon and Wendy Luo moved to Schuylkill County in 1983 with their three young children, Betsy, Caesar and Stella. They built a premier ophthalmology practice and raised their family in Orwigsburg.

The family shared a love of wine, and Dr. Luo saw that the county's rolling hills and soil could support hardy vinifera, the classic European grapes few growers attempt in a climate this cool. King Solomon's Vineyard was the result.

Today we grow Cabernet Franc, Pinot Noir, Merlot, Shiraz, Chardonnay, Riesling and Pinot Grigio, among others. The vines are pruned and tended by hand, the fruit is picked by hand, and every wine is made and bottled on site in small lots.
