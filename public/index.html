Our heritage

Pennsylvania provenance

Dr. Solomon and Wendy Luo came to Schuylkill County in 1983 with a vision for bringing the great European winemaking tradition to the county's rolling hills.

Raising their family over four decades in the region and growing roots in the community has symbolized the development of King Solomon's Vineyard into a sustainable winemaking operation serving the Schuylkill Valley.

Today we grow Cabernet Franc, Merlot and Chardonnay in the terroir of our own hillside, and make every wine by Old World methods. Every bottle traces its provenance to a single Pennsylvania vineyard.
